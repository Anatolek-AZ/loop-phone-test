# Sleep audio phone test: script (v2, Oct 11, 2026)

**Page:** https://anatolek-az.github.io/loop-phone-test/
**Throwaway.** It isn't linked from anywhere and is planned for deletion on **Thu Oct 15, 2026**.

The steps now live **on the page itself**: each card has a plain question, 1–2 numbered steps, one big button, and **It worked / It didn't**. This file is only a reference copy of the same steps. Answers are saved on the phone (localStorage), so a reload doesn't lose them.

**Before you start:** volume up, ringer on, charger plugged in. Pick a sound under **Sound to test** (it's used by every step). Total time is about 50 minutes; the quick steps come first.

| Step | Question on the card | What to do | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Do the play/pause buttons show on the lock screen? | Tap **Start sound**, lock the phone. Check for the sound's name and picture; tap Pause, wait 10 s, tap Play. | ~2 min |
| 2 | Does the moving picture loop smoothly? | Tap **Play picture and sound**; watch until the counter reaches the loop point (no jump). Lock a few seconds, unlock: the picture should move again. | ~5 min |
| 3 | Does the sleep timer fade out and stop? | Tap **Start 15-min sleep timer**, lock the phone, leave it. It should fade gently, then go silent. | ~16 min |
| 4 | Does sound keep playing when the phone is locked? | Tap **Play all night**, lock for at least 10 min, unlock. The card shows the page's own check (✅/❌). | ~10 min |
| 5 | Does a custom-length timer fade and stop? | Leave the length at 15 min, tap **Start custom timer**, lock. It fades over the last 3 min and goes silent at the time shown. | ~15 min |
| 6 | Does it play with no internet? | Tap **Download this sound**, wait for "Downloaded". Airplane Mode on, swipe Safari closed, reopen the page, tap **Play downloaded sound**. | ~5 min |
| Optional | Does it also work as a Home Screen app? | Share → Add to Home Screen → open **Loop Test** from the icon and repeat Step 4. | ~10 min |

**At the end:** tap **Send results** in the bottom bar. On the iPhone this opens the share sheet with a plain-text summary (answers, notes and the page's measurements). Send it to El Jefe. If sharing isn't available, the text is copied and shown in a box to paste. Nothing is uploaded to any server.

The bottom bar's **Step X of 6 · Next →** jumps to the next card.

### Engineer notes
- Every card has a closed **Engineer details** toggle with the old readouts (mode, readyState, drift, keep-alive A/B/C, persist(), service worker, logs). The detailed 28-item checklist and the full event log are under the Results card's details.
- Step 5 keep-alive: if it fails with **A** (default), repeat with **B**, then **C** (select is in Engineer details). Pausing moves the fade later by the pause length.
- Step 3 also has 30-min and "Cancel → all-night" buttons in its details. Video quality (720p default / 1080p) is in the Sound card's details.
- `?fast=1` (debug only) maps 1 min to 1 s in Step 5.
- Android Chrome: same cards; lock-screen controls show in the notification shade.
- Nothing in this script was run on a real phone by the box; it only ran headless Chrome checks (loop seam and fast fade in all 3 keep-alive modes).
